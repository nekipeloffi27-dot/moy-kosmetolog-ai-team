# Moodboard — moy-kosmetolog

Curated references. For each: what we take, what we don't.

## aman.com (Aman Resorts)

**Take**: premium calm without coldness, warm photography, serif headlines
with generous letter-spacing, deep emerald accent on cream, no clutter, the
courage to leave whole sections nearly empty.

**Don't take**: high luxury inaccessibility, English-only formality.

## glossier.com

**Take**: warm nude palette as primary brand, friendly intimate tone of voice,
beauty without sterility, the way they shoot products like they belong on a
real shelf in a real bathroom.

**Don't take**: pink-on-pink-on-pink saturation, ecommerce-heavy patterns.

## aesop.com

**Take**: editorial layout, restraint, premium-but-approachable, body text
that feels like real writing not marketing copy, serif everything.

**Don't take**: clinical apothecary feel that would read as "medical" in our
context.

## mejuri.com

**Take**: clean e-commerce with personality, soft warm aesthetics, the way
they integrate user photos without it feeling like a "testimonials section".

**Don't take**: ecommerce-first thinking — we're booking, not commerce.

## treatwell.com

**Take**: this is the only direct functional reference — they DO booking for
beauty. Look at their booking flow, time slot selection, confirmation, master
profile. Take the patterns, not the visual style.

**Don't take**: their visual language, which is too SaaS-default.

## calm.com (mobile app)

**Take**: soothing visual language at the app level, dark mode with warm
character (not pure black), how they make timer/clock UI feel calming
instead of clinical. The way nothing competes for attention on each screen.

**Don't take**: meditation-domain decorations.

## headspace.com

**Take**: illustration style for empty states, simple character work in line
with warm palette, onboarding flow with one decision per screen.

**Don't take**: orange branding, mascot personality.

## linear.app (only for product UI)

**Take**: density without crowding for power-user screens (the master's
dashboard, admin panels). Keyboard-shortcut respect. The way action lives on
the right with content on the left.

**Don't take**: dark-mode-first / cyberpunk feel — we're warm.

## How to use this moodboard

If you, the Designer agent, can't justify a choice ("why this color?",
"why this layout?") by pointing to a specific reference here, the choice is
probably AI-default. Reach back to a reference and reground the decision.
